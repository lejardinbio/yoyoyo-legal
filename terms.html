Yoyoyo offers recipes whose quantities are adjusted to the carbs, fat and protein you choose, a cooking mode, and tools to manage your stock and shopping list. By using the app, you accept these terms.

2. Free and Premium

  • Free: 5 adjusted meals per week (reset on Monday), a selection of recipes, 10 favourites and 7 days of history.
  • Premium: unlimited adjusted meals, all recipes, barcode scanning and branded products, automatic stock update, unlimited favourites and history.
