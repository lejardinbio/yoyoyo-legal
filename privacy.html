Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Yoyoyo helps you find a meal that fits your carbs, fat and protein. This page explains, simply, what data the app uses, where it goes and how to delete it.

Key points

  • No sign-up: no name, no password. The app creates an anonymous account on its own for backup. No email is requested unless you choose “Sign in with Apple”.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • Your stock, shopping list, favourites, history and settings are saved on your iPhone and backed up online. Your allergies and diet stay on your iPhone.
  • The camera is only used to read barcodes; no photo is saved or sent.

Who is responsible

The app is published by Louis Quemeneur, Paris (France), the data controller. Contact: support.louisapps@gmail.com.

Data saved on your phone

This information is saved on your device and is only used to run the app. Except for your allergies, diet, foods you avoid and favourite cuisines, it is also backed up to your anonymous account (see below):

  • your starting macros and settings (appearance, language, servings, equipment);
  • your allergies, diet, foods you avoid and favourite cuisines, used so you are never offered a recipe that does not suit you;
  • your stock, shopping list, favourites and history of cooked recipes;
  • the number of meals found this week (free version).

Your allergies and diet may reveal information about your health or beliefs. Providing them is optional and they do not leave your phone.

Anonymous account and backup

On first launch, the app creates an anonymous account (a technical identifier only, without name or email), hosted by Supabase in the European Union. It automatically backs up your stock, shopping list, favourites, history and settings, and the number of meals found this week (free version). Your allergies, diet, foods you avoid and favourite cuisines are not backed up. Each account can only read its own data.

“Sign in with Apple” is optional: it links this account to your Apple Account so you can get your data back on another iPhone. Yoyoyo then receives an identifier provided by Apple; if you choose to hide your email, Apple only shares a relay address.

Premium subscription

Payments are processed by Apple. To know whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and the purchase information provided by Apple (plan, dates). Yoyoyo never sees your payment details.

Statistics

The app sends anonymous statistics to PostHog, hosted in the European Union, so we can see what works and what gets in the way. You can turn them off at any time in Settings › Data and privacy › Anonymous statistics.

  • What is sent: the app being opened, the onboarding step shown, a meal search (without your macros), the recipe opened or cooked (its name in the catalogue), the number of ingredients added to shopping, the Premium screen being shown and the result of a purchase. With each event: the language, the app version and whether Premium is active.
  • What is never sent: your stock, your shopping list, your macros, your allergies, your diet, your name, your email.
  • Anonymous: the identifier is random, created at install, and not linked to your account or your subscription. No ads, no profile, no tracking across apps.

Legal basis

  • To run the app and your subscription: performance of the service you ask for.
  • For your allergies and diet: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by deleting them.
  • For anonymous statistics: our legitimate interest in improving the app; you can object by turning them off in Settings.

How long

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or remove the app. Your online backup stays until you delete your account (in the app or on request).

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Data and privacy → Clear history, Empty At home, Empty the shopping list.
  • In the app: Settings → Data and privacy → Delete my account. Your account and your whole online backup are deleted immediately.
  • Deleting the app removes what is saved on the phone, but not your online backup.
  • You can also write to support.louisapps@gmail.com.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (and similar laws where you live), you can access, correct, delete or export your data, object to its processing or withdraw your consent by writing to support.louisapps@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Minimum age

Yoyoyo is intended for people aged 15 and over (or the age of digital consent in your country, if higher, unless a parent agrees).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page is updated, and important changes are shown in the app.
