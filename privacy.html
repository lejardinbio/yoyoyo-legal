Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Yoyoyo helps you find a meal that fits your carbs, fat and protein. This page explains, simply, what data the app uses, where it goes and how to delete it.

In short

  • No account is required: no name, no email, no password.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • Your stock, shopping list, favourites, history and preferences are saved on your iPhone.
  • The camera is only used to read barcodes; no photo is saved or sent.

Who is responsible

The app is published by Louis Quemeneur, Paris (France), the data controller. Contact: support.louisapps@gmail.com.

Data saved on your phone

This information stays on your device and is only used to run the app:

  • your starting macros and settings (appearance, language, servings, equipment);
  • your allergies, diet, foods you avoid and favourite cuisines — used so you are never offered a recipe that does not suit you;
  • your stock, shopping list, favourites and history of cooked recipes;
  • the number of meals found this week (free version).

Your allergies and diet may reveal information about your health or beliefs. Providing them is optional and they do not leave your phone.

Anonymous account and backup

To back up your data and find it again on a new iPhone, the app may create an anonymous account (a technical identifier only, without name or email) hosted by Supabase in the European Union. “Sign in with Apple” is optional. Each account can only read its own data.

Premium subscription

Payments are processed by Apple. To know whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and the purchase information provided by Apple (plan, dates). Yoyoyo never sees your payment details.

Statistics

The app sends no usage statistics. If anonymous statistics were ever added, they would be off by default and would never include your meals or stock.

Legal basis

  • To run the app and your subscription: performance of the service you ask for.
  • For your allergies and diet: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by deleting them.

How long

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or remove the app. Account data is deleted on request, or after 24 months of inactivity.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Data and privacy → Clear history, Empty At home, Empty the shopping list.
  • Deleting the app removes everything saved on the phone.
  • For an account: write to support.louisapps@gmail.com.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (and similar laws where you live), you can access, correct, delete or export your data, object to its processing or withdraw your consent by writing to support.louisapps@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Minimum age

Yoyoyo is intended for people aged 15 and over (or the age of digital consent in your country, if higher, unless a parent agrees).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page is updated, and important changes are shown in the app.
